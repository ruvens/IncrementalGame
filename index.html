Timepieces

Timepieces reset each loop. Every 25 of a kind doubles its output.

Echoes carry over between loops. Spend them on permanent upgrades.

Collapse the timeline: lose all echoes and echo upgrades, and gain Paradox Shards. Shards you have ever earned permanently multiply echo gains by √(1 + shards).

Each feat permanently gives +3% moment production.

The game saves automatically every 10 seconds. It keeps running offline for up to 2 hours. Shortcuts: Space gather, R rewind, 1–7 buy timepieces.
